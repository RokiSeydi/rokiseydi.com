Roki Seydi

Computational human context · Behavioural agents · Sociotechnical AI safety · Healthcare AI

Through line

AI systems encounter people through data — records, clinical notes, interaction logs. The assumptions embedded in those representations about who people are, how they behave, and what they need are almost never tested against humans as they actually are.

Research interest

I study computational human context: how lived experience, relational history, and social position can be encoded without flattening people into static attributes or generic personas, and how context-grounded agents can expose failures in AI systems and sociotechnical environments that benchmarks cannot reach.

My work

Healthcare is my primary domain. My work identified cultural confabulation — a previously uncharacterised failure mode in frontier model reasoning for healthcare — and built open-source evaluation infrastructure on the UK AI Safety Institute's Inspect platform to measure it systematically across frontier models. I am also building ELOST, a care pathway simulation engine for stress-testing clinical AI against computational patient models grounded in lived experience.

Background
